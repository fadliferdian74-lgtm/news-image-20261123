## Dropped or replaced during verification

The research pack supplied 25 stories; four failed verification and
were replaced, and two more were reframed. The replacements above are
numbered 5 (Libya), 18 (Iowa), 19 (schools) and 21 (Brent); the
reframes are 3 (Dymer) and 16 (Taiwan).

- **"OPEC agrees to extend oil output cuts" — DROPPED, false.** No such
  decision was taken on 23 November; the next OPEC+ meeting is on
  29 November. Replaced by the Brent slide (story 21).
- **"Democrats gain 258 seats, maintaining their majority" — DROPPED.**
  The 258-seat milestone was reached and first reported on Sunday
  22 November (Chuck Hubbard's win in North Carolina), and the
  Democrats *won* the majority rather than maintaining it. Replaced
  by the Iowa lead-azide order (story 18).
- **"Trump issues executive order on federal contractors" — DROPPED.**
  Executive Order 14438 was signed on Friday 20 November and first
  reported on 21 November; it is not 23 November news. Replaced by
  the German and Danish school bomb threats (story 19).
- **"Poland says Russia tried to assassinate Ukrainian rapper twice"
  — DROPPED.** Poland's confirmation to AP was made on Sunday
  22 November. Replaced by the Libya warship story (story 5).
- **Dymer — REFRAMED and enriched.** The strike was on Sunday
  22 November; Monday's news was the toll reaching 11 and the two
  rescues. Typeset with the enriched facts: Valentyna, 79, rescued
  after 22 hours; Davyd, 7, after 14 hours.
- **Taiwan — REFRAMED.** The Meta-accounts story was first reported
  on 20 November. Typeset instead as Monday's warning by Taiwan's
  Mainland Affairs Council of Chinese interference ahead of
  Saturday's local elections.
- **CrowdStrike/Zscaler "$10bn" detail — KEPT, verified.** The
  companies said the combined firm is expected to begin with more
  than $10bn in annual recurring revenue at closing.
- **Milkita factory fire — REFRAMED as Monday's confirmation.** The
  fire broke out on Saturday 21 November; the 24-dead toll and the
  ~30 missing were confirmed by Indonesian authorities on Monday.
